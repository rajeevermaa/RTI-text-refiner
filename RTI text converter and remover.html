3. What was found and changed

Kept as they are: letters, digits, spaces, line breaks (paragraphs) and , . - _ ( ) / @ : & ? \ %

Replaced with an allowed equivalent:
  • Hidden or odd spaces become a normal space. Tabs become a space.
  • Bullet points (• ● ▪ ➤ * etc.) at the start of a line become list labels. If the text already uses Roman numbering (i, ii, iii), labels become (A), (B), (C). If it already uses letters, labels become (i), (ii), (iii). If it uses both, labels become Item A, Item B. If neither, (A), (B), (C). Each separate list restarts from the beginning.
  • + becomes plus, = becomes equals, < and > become less than and greater than, ± becomes plus or minus, × becomes x.
  • ₹ becomes Rs, $ becomes USD, € becomes EUR, £ becomes GBP, # becomes No., ° becomes degrees.
  • Semicolon becomes a comma. ! becomes a full stop. [ ] and { } become ( ). Long dashes become -. The ellipsis character becomes ... . Fractions like ½ become 1/2.
Removed (no allowed equivalent): quotation marks, apostrophes, zero-width and other hidden characters, emojis, non-English letters and any other unsupported symbol.

Your text stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
